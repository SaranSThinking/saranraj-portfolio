Personal Projects for Practical Learning

Real formulas, applied to everyday problems - built so students (and I) can experience the concepts by using them, not just reading about them.

Every project below takes a technique from Industrial Engineering, Management, or AI & ML and turns it into something you'd actually use on yourself: your pantry, your habits, your study routine, your day. The math is the same math taught in the domain pages - this is where it gets hands dirty.
