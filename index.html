About

Academic leadership, grounded in industry-facing research

I teach and coordinate UG/PG courses in Operations, Business Analytics, Industrial Engineering, and Quality Engineering at CEG, Anna University - building outcome-based lesson plans, digital content, and gamified learning aligned to NBA standards. Alongside teaching, I bring hands-on NBA & NAAC accreditation expertise - coordinating peer-team-visit readiness, documentation, and department-level audits - and run national symposiums (INDCON) and academic–industry collaboration workshops on Industry 4.0, Lean Six Sigma, and analytics.

📋 Full DMAIC walkthrough

DMAIC Case Study: Engine Assembly Line Productivity

Lean Six Sigma

Walkthrough of the productivity-improvement study on my CV, using the Maynard Operation Sequence Technique (MOST) to rebalance an engine assembly line.

D

Define

Engine assembly line was missing takt-time targets; cycle time imbalance across stations flagged as the core productivity constraint.

M

Measure

Station-wise cycle times captured and decomposed into elemental motions using MOST, establishing a baseline standard time per station.

A

Analyze

Line-balancing analysis identified bottleneck stations and non-value-adding motion sequences driving cycle-time variance.

I

Improve

Work elements re-sequenced and reallocated across stations using MOST-derived standard times to balance the line to takt time.

C

Control

Revised SOPs and station standard times documented for sustained line balance and future audits.
