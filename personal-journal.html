Personal Journal

A reflection journal that pulls a live snapshot from every other tool on this page — supply chain, quality control, study, productivity, breakeven, decisions, and ergonomics — into each entry.

The other six personal projects each generate a number. This is where the numbers and the reflection meet — so "why did I decide that" has the data sitting right next to it.

The Problem.

// data in one tab, reflection in another

Every tool above this one generates a real number — an OEE score, a control-chart flag, a regression fit, a decision ranking. But that number lives and dies in its own tool. By the time you sit down to reflect on your week, you've forgotten what the numbers actually said.

This project closes that loop: writing a journal entry can pull today's numbers from every other tool automatically, so the reflection and the evidence sit in the same place.

02 /

Methodology.

// read-only snapshot at write-time

Shared Local Storage

Every personal-project tool on this site writes its results to the browser's Web Storage. This journal reads (never writes to) those same keys.

Lightweight Recompute

Rather than trusting stale saved summaries, the journal recomputes each tool's headline metric (OEE average, control-chart flags, regression R², etc.) fresh, every time.

Tag-Based Organization

Entries are tagged by which part of your life they're about, so you can filter your history down to just "Study" or just "Ergonomics" reflections.

Graceful Absence

If a given tool has no data yet, it's simply omitted from the snapshot — nothing breaks, and the journal works from day one even before you've touched the other six tools.

What’s in the build.

Attach a live read of all seven tools to any entry with a single checkbox.

Live dashboard preview

See your current cross-tool snapshot any time, without writing an entry.

Eight reflection tags

Supply Chain, Quality, Study, Productivity, Breakeven, Decisions, Ergonomics, and General.

Filterable timeline

Browse your full journal or filter down to entries about one specific tool.

Sample entries

Three illustrative entries load instantly so you can see the format before writing your own.

Local persistence

Your journal is saved in the browser between visits — nothing is sent anywhere, including the snapshots.
