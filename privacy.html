Policy

Privacy

A short, plain account of what this site does with your data. It is a personal academic portfolio, so there is very little.

Analytics

This site uses Cloudflare Web Analytics to count visits and see which pages are read. It reports aggregate figures only. It does not set advertising cookies, and I do not build profiles of individual visitors.

Calculators and tools

The interactive tools in the Expertise and Learn by Doing sections run entirely in your browser. The numbers you type in are not sent to me or to any server.

Some Learn by Doing tools keep a local log so your entries survive a page refresh. That log is stored in your browser's local storage on your own device. Clearing your browser data removes it.

Class assignment pages

The pages under /assignments are built for students enrolled in my courses. Where a page asks you to sign in with a register number, your name, register number and progress may be recorded to a course tracking form so I can follow coursework. These pages are for enrolled students only.

Contact and links

There are no contact forms. If you email me, I use your message only to reply. Links to LinkedIn, ChatGPT and other sites lead to services with their own privacy policies.

Questions or removal requests

Write to fencer.saran@gmail.com and I will respond.
