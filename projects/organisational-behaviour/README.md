# Organisational Behaviour

**Live:** https://saransthinking.github.io/saranraj-portfolio/projects/organisational-behaviour/
**Part of:** [Saranraj Ramachandran — Industrial Engineering Portfolio](https://saransthinking.github.io/saranraj-portfolio/)

Team dynamics, leadership, and change management — tested on the shop floor and on the competition floor. This
domain draws as much from captaining a national fencing team and coaching over 70 athletes as it does from the
classroom — leadership and team performance under pressure are the same problem whether the "shop floor" is a
factory or a competition mat.

## Tools

### Leadership Style Self-Assessment
Rates how strongly each of six statements reflects your default approach (1–5), scoring three classic
leadership styles — Directive, Participative, Delegative — and identifying the primary lean.

### Team Development Stage Finder
Select the behavior that best matches a team right now to identify its Tuckman stage (Forming, Storming,
Norming, Performing, Adjourning), with a specific action tip for that stage.
