Case Studies

Published & Presented Work

Conference · 2019

Modelling & Analysis of Ergonomic Variables in the IT Industry

ICBAI International Conference, IISc Bangalore

Used Interpretive Structural Modelling (ISM) and Structural Equation Modelling (SEM) to map how ergonomic variables in IT workplaces relate to and influence one another, identifying which factors act as root drivers versus downstream symptoms.

Methods: ISM, SEM

Conference · 2022

Quantifying & Prioritizing Ergonomic Measures in an Automobile Industry

ICAIEA International Conference, DoIE, CEG, Anna University

Applied Analytic Network Process (ANP) and DEMATEL to rank candidate ergonomic interventions on an automobile shop floor by impact and interdependency, giving plant management a prioritized action list rather than a flat checklist.

Methods: ANP, DEMATEL

Journal · Communicated

Ergonomic Effect on Manufacturing Industries’ Productivity - A Review

R. Saranraj and Dr. T. Ramesh Babu

A literature review consolidating how ergonomic risk factors - posture, workload, environment - translate into measurable productivity loss in manufacturing settings, framing ergonomics as a productivity lever rather than only a compliance concern.

Focus: Ergonomics–productivity linkage
